How it works

Hold both sides. Keep only the gap.

You deposit USDCx. The vault opens equal-notional pairs — short the perp, long the spot — so price moves cancel and hourly funding is all that's left. Only what's actually received accrues to NAV. You redeem at a higher NAV.

1

Deposit

Deposit USDCx into the vault and receive shares at NAV. You are the holder.

2

Open the pair

Short BTC/ETH perp on Hyperliquid + long cBTC/cETH custodied on Canton, equal notional — net delta 0, enforced on-chain.

3

Collect funding

Hourly funding is marked to an oracle feed and credited to NAV — realized only. If trailing funding decays, the sign guard unwinds both legs: the short never pays rent.

4

Redeem

Redeem anytime at the higher NAV/share. Every step is on-chain and auditable.
